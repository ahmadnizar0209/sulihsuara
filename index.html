Sulih Suara records the sound of any app on your Mac — a call, a stream, a browser tab, a music app — or everything the Mac plays, and writes it straight to a file.

What it does

  • Capture one app's audio, or System Audio, with macOS audio taps. Nothing to install, no extra drivers.
  • Add microphones and audio interfaces to the same session.
  • Shape the signal on the Audio Grid: gain, EQ, compressor, limiter, noise reduction, declick and channel mixer.
  • Record to M4A (AAC), MP3, ALAC, FLAC, WAV or AIFF, with automatic file names and splitting for long recordings.
  • Schedule recordings to start and stop on their own while Sulih Suara is open.

Try it free

Setting up sessions is always free. Start the free 14-day trial to record with every feature at full quality, then unlock the Full Version once — no subscription.

Requires macOS 14.4 or later. Available on the Mac App Store.
