Privacy Policy

Sulih Suara does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • Audio is captured and processed on your Mac. Recordings are written only to the folders you choose (by default, Music › Sulih Suara).
  • Sessions, schedules and settings are stored on your Mac, inside the app's own container.
  • There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app makes no network connections of its own.
  • The trial and the Full Version are handled by the Mac App Store. Apple processes the purchase; the developer receives no personal or payment information.
  • The name shown as “This Application Developed for …” is your Mac account's name. It is read on your Mac and never leaves it.
  • A support report is created only when you ask for one, you see its contents first, and it is never sent automatically.
