Getting started

Click New Session, add a source (an app, System Audio or a microphone) and a Recorder, then press Record (⌘R). Stop with ⌘. (Command-period).

macOS asks for permission

The first recording asks to allow Sulih Suara to record audio from other apps, and the microphone if you use one. If you declined, turn it on in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

Where are my recordings?

By default in your Music folder, in Sulih Suara. Each Recorder block can write somewhere else: click it and choose a folder.

An app can't be captured

A few apps don't allow their audio to be tapped on its own. Use a System Audio source instead; it records everything the Mac plays except Sulih Suara.

Scheduled recordings didn't start

Schedules run while Sulih Suara is open and the Mac is awake. Turn on Open at Login in Settings › Schedules.

Trial and purchases

Start the free trial or unlock the Full Version in Settings › Purchase. To move a purchase to another Mac signed in to the same Apple Account, choose Restore Purchases there. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.
